## Things to note

1. **The OSM'26 "Upcoming Events" are all past** (21–27 February 2026).
2. **The organization isn't the NGO the brief describes.** The brief says "NGO in ECOSOC consultative status." The site says "UN Decade Coordination Office - Ocean Observing" and ties it to GOOS and the Ocean Decade. The "UN status" component and logo rules need to follow that, not ECOSOC wording.
3. **The header logo is the UN Ocean Decade logo itself.** The Ocean Decade has brand rules for how Decade Actions and offices use it.
4. **Both photos are stock:** an Unsplash photo by Matt Hardy, and a Sofar Ocean buoy photo.
5. **The social icons are Squarespace placeholders.**
6. **Where contact-form submissions go is unknown** from the outside; it's set inside Squarespace.
